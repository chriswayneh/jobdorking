How to search LinkedIn jobs with Google

Use Google to narrow LinkedIn job listings by title, recency, and the terms that matter to your search.

Written by Chris Hickman · Published August 21, 2026

Start with the jobs section

Restricting a search to LinkedIn’s jobs area keeps the results focused on listings rather than profiles, posts, and company pages. Add a quoted title when the title is specific, then adjust the wording if results are too narrow.

Recent product-manager roles

site:linkedin.com/jobs intitle:"Product Manager" after:2026-08-14

Remote frontend roles

site:linkedin.com/jobs intitle:"Frontend Engineer" remote after:2026-08-14

Refine one variable at a time

Begin with title and date. Add skills, location, or employment words only after you see the baseline results. A query is a filter, not a guarantee: Google can only return pages it has indexed, and a listing may have changed since it was crawled.

  • Use quotation marks for titles with several words.
  • Use after: with an ISO date to prioritize newer indexed pages.
  • Try title alternatives with OR, such as ("Product Manager" OR "Product Owner").
