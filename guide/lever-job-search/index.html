How to find Lever jobs with Google

Search across public Lever-hosted openings with a role, a date window, and the language employers actually use.

Written by Chris Hickman · Published August 21, 2026

Target public Lever listings

Lever job boards commonly publish openings at jobs.lever.co. A Google site: operator lets you search those pages as a group, then use the role title to focus results.

Recent data-analyst listings

site:jobs.lever.co intitle:"Data Analyst" after:2026-08-14

Senior remote engineering roles

site:jobs.lever.co intitle:"Software Engineer" senior remote after:2026-08-14

Match the employer’s wording

Role titles vary. Test adjacent titles such as "Data Analyst" OR "Business Analyst", or remove quotes if an exact title produces too few results. Use a skill or technology term only when it is genuinely required for your search.

Keep results current

Use after: to prioritize recently indexed pages, but always confirm the employer’s posted date and application status. An indexed result can remain visible after a company closes the opening.
